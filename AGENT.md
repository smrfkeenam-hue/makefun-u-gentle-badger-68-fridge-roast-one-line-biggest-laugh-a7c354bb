# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun bounty `u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb`
("Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh", $5 top 3).

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes a single roast line, max 200 characters, to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

The line roasts the smart fridge itself — its firmware update, its reboot,
its ice maker, and its app — and lands on a fresh angle: the ice maker
holding cubes hostage until the app gets a five-star rating. No trailing
newline.
